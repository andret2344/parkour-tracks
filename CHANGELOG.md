# ParkourTracks changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- ParkourTracks, the successor of atsParkour and atsQuickParkour, rewritten for Paper 26.2: tracks built from WorldEdit
  regions with a spawn, checkpoints, a finish and walls, a timer counted in ticks, medals, records, a sidebar, record
  signs, a track selection menu, boat tracks, fees and rewards through Vault, and events for other plugins.
